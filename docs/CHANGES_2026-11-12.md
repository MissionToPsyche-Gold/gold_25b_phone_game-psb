# Changes on November 12, 2026 (UI update + tests)

Read this before merging, especially the files under **"Teammates' files I touched"**.

## New
- **Automated tests:** GUT 9.7.1 in `addons/gut/`, with 9 suites and 33 test cases in `tests/unit/` and `tests/integration/`. See `docs/TESTING.md`.
- **About page:** `scenes/about.tscn`, opened from a new **About** button on the main menu. It has three sections: the team, "About this game", and "The Psyche mission" (facts from the 2023 press kit). The text is in `scripts/ui/about_info.gd`. The team decided not to include advisors, resources, credits or the disclaimer there.
- **Callsigns instead of typed names:** players flip through ready-made NASA/Psyche names (`scripts/ui/callsigns.gd`), and no two players can share one. This replaces typed nicknames (sponsor request), so **report requirements UF-2, SF03 and SF04 need rewording.**
- **Question pictures:** a question in `data/trivia.json` can have `"image": "res://path/to/picture.png"`, and the trivia screen shows it above the answers. Question 104 (potato shape) uses the Psyche sprite as a demo.
- **Mission story for minigames:** each entry in `GameStateUI.MINIGAMES` has a `"story"`, shown on the challenge intro. The maze is now "Race to Psyche".

## Changed look
- **Turquoise is gone.** The info accent is now Psyche brand **Magenta**, and the palette follows the NASA Psyche Brand Guide (Black, Dark Purple, Purple, Gold, Mustard). Use accent `"Magenta"` in new code. `"Cyan"` still works but now draws magenta.
- **Player colors:** orange, blue, pink, green.
- **Spacecraft sprite:** redrawn with two five-panel **cross-shaped** solar arrays, matching the press kit.
- **Title font:** Jersey 10. The old font's C looked like an O.
- The main-menu footer moved to the About page.
